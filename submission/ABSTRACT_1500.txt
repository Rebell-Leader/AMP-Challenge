We treat AMP library design as a scoring-alignment problem, not a generator problem.

Generation: an order-3 Markov model trained on 40,047 real AMPs (MarLys antibacterial subset plus DBAASP/QMAP peptides with panel MIC <= 16 uM) draws a 1,000,000-candidate pool under a fixed seed. Pure NumPy, no GPU.

The decisive step is selection. We reproduced the evaluation stack locally - seqme plus the three AMP oracles in its plugin registry (amPEPpy, AMPredictor, AMPlify) - and scored our previously locked library against real potent AMPs, UniProt non-AMPs and shuffled-AMP decoys. It sat at the decoy floor on predicted potency and embedding realism: an order-3 Markov chain over AMPs is, to a global-sequence oracle, close to a smart shuffle of AMPs. Diversity and novelty, where it looked strong, were never the binding components.

We therefore select the 50,000 members by amPEPpy/AMPredictor rank consensus within length strata, holding AMPlify out as an independent judge. Versus the locked library, 8 of 11 seqme components improve, 2 hold, and only diversity trades down (0.855 -> 0.816), to the real-potent-AMP level of 0.820. Held-out AMPlify rises 0.577 -> 0.907, so the gain is not scorer-specific.

The top-100 uses a four-judge consensus (three oracles plus an in-house ESM-2/XGBoost MIC ensemble) under novelty, hemolysis and diversity gates: amPEPpy 0.905, AMPlify 1.000, AMPredictor median MIC 1.11 uM, worst reference identity 0.74. Outputs regenerate byte-for-byte from seed 2027.
